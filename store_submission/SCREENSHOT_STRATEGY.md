# Screenshot plan

Play needs at least 2 and allows up to 8 phone screenshots. Each side must be 320 to 3,840 px, and the long side can be at most twice the short side. JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha channel. These limits were read from several current guides on 1 November 2026. Google's own page is the authority, so check it at upload.

## Your phone's screenshots need cropping

Your device captures 720 x 1640, a ratio of 2.28:1, which is over the 2:1 limit and would be rejected.

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Crop | Remove about 120 px from the top (status bar) and about 120 px from the bottom (navigation bar). The result is 720 x 1400, a ratio of 1.94:1. |
| Why also the status bar | It shows your notifications, battery and carrier, which do not belong in a listing |
| Quality | Upscaling to 1080 x 2100 is acceptable. Do not stretch. |

## What may appear in them

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Use only royalty-free or your own photos and videos, placed in a folder named `.Statuses` by you | Real people's statuses are private and may be copyrighted |
| No faces of identifiable people unless they gave permission | Privacy and policy risk |
| No messaging-service name, logo, colour scheme or interface in the images or captions | Impersonation and trademark policy |
| No claims such as "without being seen" | Metadata policy |
| Show only features that exist in the build | Play rejects misleading assets |

## Capturing

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build | Use the release or test build that matches what you submit |
| Folder | Create `.Statuses` on the phone, put your sample media in it, and pick it in the app |
| Mode | Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification appears |
| Order | Take them light mode first, then dark for #6 |
| Check | After cropping, open each one and look for anything personal |
