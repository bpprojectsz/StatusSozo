Answers to common questions

How do I connect the status folder?

Tap Choose folder. In the system picker, check that you are in the status folder, then tap Use this folder and Allow. The folder must be named .Statuses. If the app says it is the wrong folder, choose the folder with that exact name.

The picker does not show the folder.

Open the picker's menu and turn on Show hidden files, because the status folder is hidden. Then browse to it and tap Use this folder.

Why does nothing show up?

A status only appears after you have viewed it in the messaging app, and the messaging app must be installed on this phone. Open a status, come back to StatusSozo and tap Refresh.

Where do saved files go?

Photos are saved to Pictures/StatusSozo and videos to Movies/StatusSozo, so they also appear in your gallery app.

What happens after I reinstall the app?

Files you saved stay in your gallery. The new installation does not own them, so they are not listed on the Saved screen. You can still open, share or delete them from your gallery app.

How do I disconnect a folder?

Open Settings, tap the folder under Folders, then tap Disconnect. You can also remove access in your phone's system settings.

Can I delete a saved item from the app?

Yes, from the Saved screen. If Android refuses, remove the item from your gallery app instead.

How do I report a problem?

Open Settings and tap Send feedback, or email stmakarios@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and Android version.

Is StatusSozo affiliated with a messaging service?

No. StatusSozo is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any messaging service. Only save and share content you have the right to use.
