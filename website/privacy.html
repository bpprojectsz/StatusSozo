Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

StatusSozo is an Android app that lets you browse, save and share status photos and videos from a folder you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect your personal data or upload your files, and the ads it shows are provided by Google.

What StatusSozo collects

StatusSozo itself collects nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and the developer does not receive or store any of your data on a server. The advertising service the app uses does handle some information, as described under Advertising below.

Network access

StatusSozo uses the internet only to show ads and to ask for your advertising choices, through Google's services. Your photos, videos and folders are never uploaded. Everything else happens on your device, and the app works without a connection; the ads simply do not appear.

What the app reads

The app reads only the folder you select through the Android system folder picker. It does not access your chats, contacts, messages or any other folder. You can remove the access at any time by choosing Disconnect in the app's Settings, or through your phone's system settings.

What the app writes

When you tap Save, the app copies the chosen item into your gallery: photos to Pictures/StatusSozo and videos to Movies/StatusSozo. When you share an item, the app makes a temporary copy in its private cache so the share sheet can read it. Temporary copies are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores your theme choice, your language choice, your last selected source, the folder access you granted, and a count of how many full-screen ads were shown today (so they are not shown too often), on your device only. This information never leaves your phone.

Feedback and problem reports

If you tap Send feedback in Settings, or Report a problem, the app opens your email app with a message addressed to the support address. The message can include the app version and a short diagnostic log kept only on your device. You can read, edit or delete all of it before you send. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app.

Advertising

StatusSozo is free and shows ads. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. The app shows a banner at the bottom of some screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after an action such as saving several items. Full-screen ads are limited in how often they appear, and never appear when the app opens or closes or while a video is playing.

To show ads, Google's advertising software may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address, information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive this information. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before it requests any ad. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised, and sometimes there are none to show. Google decides that, not the app. If Google's consent form cannot be shown, for example because there is no connection, the app still asks Google for ads and Google applies your last saved choice. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices row in the app's Settings, which appears wherever the law requires it. On any phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, then Ads). The app asks the ad network to show only content suitable for a general audience.

A future paid option to remove ads is planned. If it is added, this policy will be updated before it is released.

Children

StatusSozo is not directed to children under 13. The developer collects no personal data from anyone.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app removes its settings and temporary files. Items you saved remain in your gallery, because they are ordinary pictures and videos. After a reinstall, previously saved items are not listed in the app's Saved screen, but you can still find them in your gallery.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
