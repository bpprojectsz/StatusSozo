Privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026

StatusSozo is an Android app that lets you browse, save and share status photos and videos you have already viewed, from a folder you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects none.

What StatusSozo collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Network access

The current version of StatusSozo does not connect to the internet and does not request the internet permission. Everything happens on your device.

What the app reads

The app reads only the folder you select through the Android system folder picker. It does not access your chats, contacts, messages or any other folder. You can remove the access at any time by choosing Disconnect in the app's Settings, or through your phone's system settings.

What the app writes

When you tap Save, the app copies the chosen item into your gallery: photos to Pictures/StatusSozo and videos to Movies/StatusSozo. When you share an item, the app makes a temporary copy in its private cache so the share sheet can read it. Temporary copies are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores your theme choice, your last selected source and the folder access you granted, on your device only. This information never leaves your phone.

Advertising

The current version does not show ads. If ads are added in a future version, this policy and the information shown in the app will be updated before that version is released.

Children

StatusSozo is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app removes its settings and temporary files. Items you saved remain in your gallery, because they are ordinary pictures and videos. After a reinstall, previously saved items are not listed in the app's Saved screen, but you can still find them in your gallery.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

StatusSozo is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any messaging service.
