Keep the statuses you have already viewed.

StatusSozo shows the status photos and videos stored on your phone, so you can save the ones you want to your gallery and share them later. You choose the folder. Nothing else is read.

What it does

Browse

Photos and Videos in two tabs, newest first, with live counts.

Save

Save one item or a whole selection. Saved files appear in your gallery under Pictures and Movies.

Share

Send one or many items through the standard share sheet.

Private

No account, no tracking, no network access. Only the folder you pick is read.

How it works

  1. Open StatusSozo and tap Choose folder.
  2. Pick the status folder in the system file picker and tap Use this folder.
  3. Browse, select and save. Items you save are kept in your gallery.

Questions

Why is my list empty?

A status only appears after you have viewed it in the messaging app, and the messaging app must be installed. Open a status, then come back and refresh.

Does StatusSozo need any permissions?

No. You grant access to one folder through the system picker, and you can remove that access in Settings at any time.

Is it affiliated with a messaging service?

No. StatusSozo is independent. Only save and share content you have the right to use.
